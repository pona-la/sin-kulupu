Do you want to read new and original toki pona literature? Good news! Today's newsletter is absolutely packed with new works from the writing contest - and that's not the only thing this newsletter has to offer!

### Community projects

* [suno pi toki pona](https://bsky.app/profile/tokipona.net/post/3mq67wfgqlk24) is a 24 hour event on toki pona's birthday (August 8). The language will be a whopping 25 years old! There will be a livestream the whole day with games, booths, livestreamed music, presentations, so much good stuff - and that means your help is needed! Do you have something to contribute on the stream, either by being there live or by submitting a pre-recorded video? Would you like to help organise in some way? [Fill in this form](https://forms.gle/NiywJuGRrtPf8rWu7)!
* The annual horror zine lipu monsuta ([Discord invite](https://discord.gg/6BVwfQXhvF)) is back! This year, it will be called lape monsuta. [Submit a story or comic](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSd5LAVtOvNgoGFi5WkhWZccrWyihRr0svU1m6OvYTnmkIMzoA/viewform) until September 1st!
* jan Jun would like you to fill out a [survey about how the language is used in specific contexts](https://www.surveymonkey.com/r/HZ5XKV7)!
* There was a [picnic in Seattle](https://www.reddit.com/r/tokipona/comments/1v0a73w/seattle_toki_pona_picnic/) on July 25th. It is already over, but if that sounds like something you would have joined, ask around for the Sijatoki group!
* Meanwhile, people living in Catalonia, Spain, France or Germany under 31 [might be eligible for a grant](https://www.reddit.com/r/tokipona/comments/1uzvrbk/mani_tawa_tenpo_kulupu_pi_ma_elopa_you_might_be/) for the annual European meetup!

### utala musi pi toki pona

This year's writing (and comic drawing) contest has many many really great documents that people have created! 37 _brand-new_, original(!) pieces of toki pona literature. And now it's up to you to _read as many as you want_ and vote on the stories you've read. 

[Here is an overview](https://utala.pona.la/index_en.html) of all the stories.

And you can [vote on the documents right here](https://forms.gle/caHbNRjem1K1zW5V9)!

Useful information:
* You don't have to read everything to vote, just vote on the ones you read!
* You can update your vote! Once you read some more entries, you can vote again as often as you need!
* Voting closes on August 7, right before _suno pi toki pona_!
* Not confident in your ⚂sitelen pona skills yet? All documents are also transcribed in Latin script!
