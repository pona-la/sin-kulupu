Where would you like to go? So many options! We could go visit a library, we could watch a baseball match, you could go on a fantasy adventure as a lone wolf... or maybe you'd like to stay home, drink some nice warm liquid and read spooky stories, play videogames and listen to tunes? The choice is yours:

⚂ = sitelen pona

### Community projects

* lipu monsuta was an annual magazine filled with short horror stories. tenpo monsuta is its successor and has published their first issue: lape monsuta available as a [digital pdf](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1HmTiWk8kKaKx65cxkqBudRdexec4xxDe/view?usp=drive_link), [epub](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1PCP1rW3op6BMcmmI1ocQLun-fOWkoBrv/view?usp=drive_link) and [printable booklet](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1RfpvAQ6YBA5TRcL3TNFnKqUj9t8-YhxN/view?usp=drive_link)!
* kijetesantakalu Omi made a platform people can [share videos and other posts](https://o-pana.o-pana.workers.dev/) on!
* Back to more basic internet technology? jan Kekan San opened up [a forum](https://groups.io/g/tokipona/) that people can participate in through the website or by sending emails!
* Are you in Chemnitz, Germany, and interested in toki pona? [You're not alone!](https://www.reddit.com/r/Chemnitz/comments/1wsi0b5/toki_pona_meetup_deen/)
